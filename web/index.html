How it works

  1. 01Create

    Set an amount, or leave it open for tips. Add a memo and an optional expiry.

  2. 02Share

    Send the link in any chat, or show the QR code at your counter.

  3. 03Get paid

    Your customer pays from their wallet. USDC goes straight to yours; nothing is held in between.

When you pay

  1. 01Check

    The amount, what it is for, and the address. It is grouped in fours so you can compare it.

  2. 02Approve

    One transaction in your wallet. The network fee is paid in USDC too.

  3. 03Keep the receipt

    The terminal prints one, with a link to the transaction on Arc.

Indicator lights

Open
Live. Anyone with the link can pay it.
Paid
Paid in full. A fixed-amount link closes after its payment.
Closed
Closed by you, or past its expiry. It can no longer be paid.

Good to know

  • Links are public. Anyone with a link number can read its amount and memo on Arc, so keep private details out of memos.
  • Closing is one transaction. It only stops new payments. USDC you have received is already in your wallet.
