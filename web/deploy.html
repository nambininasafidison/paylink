This page deploys the PayLink contract from your own wallet. Your private key never leaves the wallet. You only approve one transaction, and the gas is paid in USDC (a few cents).

Before you start

  • A wallet on Arc. MetaMask or Rabby. If the wallet does not know the Arc network yet, this page asks it to add it.
  • About 1 USDC for gas. The deployment itself costs a few cents. On Arc, gas is paid in USDC, so there is no other token to buy.
  • Nothing to install. The contract is already compiled. Your wallet signs one transaction to publish it, and from then on it only routes payments: it never holds funds.
