tested in. Those are pinned by test/fuzz/Amount.t.sol::test_PaymentsCounterNeverWraps, test/unit/Precedence.t.sol and
the over-credit tests, and the hand-written mutants of src/PayLinkV2.sol (protocol/audit/README.md#mutation-testing)
