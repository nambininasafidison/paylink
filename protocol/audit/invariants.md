# PayLinkV2 invariants I1–I11: tests and evidence

> Self-review evidence, **not a third-party audit**. Source of the invariants: PAYLINK-V2-SPEC §3.3.4.

Each invariant is checked three ways:

1. **Campaign** (`test/invariant/`): a stateful, handler-based fuzzing campaign. The handler is model-based: before every call it predicts the outcome from its own independent model of the contract (success, or the exact revert data), performs the call with a low-level `call`, decodes the emitted logs into the ghost ledger, and records a violation under the invariant's ID if anything disagrees. Each invariant function (for example `invariant_I1_relativeConservation`) then asserts both the property on chain state and "no violation recorded" for its ID, so a failure is reported under the invariant's own name with the first offending call as the message.
2. **Fuzz tests** (`test/fuzz/`), for the properties that are cheaper to fuzz statelessly (time windows, amounts, domains, nonce binding).
3. **Unit tests** (`test/unit/`), one per edge case and revert path.

## Campaign setup

|---|---|
| Files | `test/invariant/Invariants.t.sol` (assertions), `Handler.sol` (actions, model, per-call checks), `GhostLedger.sol` (independent record) |
| Actors | 3 EOAs, each both payer and payee; an ERC-1271 wallet payee (`Wallet1271`, owned by actor 0); a relayer; a `Donor`. Payments, `cancel` and `cancelBySig` run under a seeded `tx.origin` (`vm.prank(sender, origin)`): the sender itself one time in three, the invoice's payee (or the wallet's owner) one time in three, otherwise the relayer or another actor |
| Assets | `Mock3009` (6 decimals, EIP-3009 + EIP-2612, USDC-like), `MockPermit` (18 decimals, EIP-2612 only, MUSD-like), `fee` (`FeeOnTransfer`, EIP-3009 + EIP-2612, 1 % on every leg), `fwdFee` (`FeeOnTransfer` with PayLink exempt as a recipient, so the 1 % falls on the EIP-3009 forward leg or the payee's credit; deduct-from-amount or charge-the-sender, switched by `reconfigureFee`; amounts up to 100 units so the fee stays within reach of the stray balance), `overCredit` (`OverCreditToken`, EIP-3009 + EIP-2612, standard until `reconfigureOverCredit` arms one leg to move 1 to 10^6 base units *more* than asked: PayLink credited extra on the receive, the payee credited extra on a pull or on the forward, or PayLink debited less on the forward), native coin |
| Deployments | the PayLinkV2 under test and an **alternate deployment** (same code, other address) for I7 |
| Actions (15 selectors) | `createInvoice`, `warp`, `payWithAuthorization` (×2 weight), `pay`, `payWithPermit` (optionally front-run by a griefer), `payNative`, `cancel` (by any actor or the wallet), `cancelBySig` (by any signer, relayed, a quarter already expired), `donate` (ERC-20 transfer or forced native balance), `relayerRedirect` (I8 attack), `crossDomainReplay` (I7 attack: other deployment or other chain id, payment or cancel), `wrongPath` (I10: wrong entry point, plain transfer, unknown selector), `reconfigureFee` (switches `fwdFee` between its two fee models), `reconfigureOverCredit` (arms `overCredit` on one leg, or disarms it) |
| Invoices | 6 seeded (one per asset, one to the wallet), plus seeded stray `fwdFee` and `overCredit` balances of 0.5 units each, then random: open or fixed amount, `validAfter` from 1 day ago to 6 h ahead, `validUntil` 0 or up to 3 days after, `maxPayments` 0–4; at most 64 per run |
| Revert policy | `fail_on_revert = true`. The handler never reverts by design, so any revert is a bug in the suite |
| Local profile | 64 runs × depth 128 = 8,192 calls |
| **CI profile** | **256 runs × depth 128 = 32,768 calls**, as the spec requires; the campaign takes about 2 min 12 s on 4 cores (the whole CI-profile suite 2 min 32 s), so no reduction was needed |

### Result, CI profile (`FOUNDRY_PROFILE=ci forge test`), 2026-10-07

The handler counts outcomes per action and asset. A representative run (local profile, last run of the campaign, `-vv`) settled 17 payments across 10 invoices and cancelled 3, and exercised: `ok` on all four payment paths (including `fwdFee` through `pay` and `payWithPermit` in its charge-the-sender mode); `WrongAmount`, `Cancelled`, `Expired`, `SoldOut`, `SelfPayment`, `WrongPaymentPath`; on the EIP-3009 path `ReceivedMismatch` from the receive-delta check (`fee`), and for `fwdFee` all three forward-leg outcomes: `PayeeShortPaid` (`_pushExact`), `ReceivedMismatch` from the conservation post-check (donations covered the fee), and the token's `ERC20InsufficientBalance` (they did not); `PayeeShortPaid` on the allowance paths; `NotPayee`, `SignatureExpired`, `InvalidSignature` (cross-domain replays), and the token's own `FiatTokenV2: invalid signature` (every relayer redirect that gets past PayLink's own checks). Each outcome matched the model's prediction byte for byte. Since the test-quality review, `overCredit` adds, on the EIP-3009 path, `ReceivedMismatch(amount, amount + bonus)` from the receive check, `PayeeShortPaid(amount, amount + bonus)` from `_pushExact` and `ReceivedMismatch(stray, stray + bonus)` from the post-check, `PayeeShortPaid(amount, amount + bonus)` from `_pullExact` on the allowance paths, and settlements on every path whose legs the armed mode leaves alone.

**Mutation evidence.** Deleting the conservation post-check (M11) or the forward-leg payee-delta check (M13) is now caught by this campaign alone at the CI profile: M11 fails `invariant_I1_relativeConservation` and `invariant_ModelAgreesWithContract` (a `fwdFee` payment settled although the token charged PayLink's forward leg); M13 fails `invariant_I6_exactDeltas` (payee delta ≠ amount) and `invariant_ModelAgreesWithContract`. Before `fwdFee`, both survived the campaign, because every EIP-3009 attempt with the only fee token stopped at the receive-delta check (1,362 `ReceivedMismatch` outcomes over 257 runs, no attempt ever reaching the forward leg). The same holds for the ten mutants of the 2026-10-07 test-quality review, which all survived the campaign before it: the CI campaign alone kills the one-sided exactness checks M50–M54 and the `tx.origin` mutants M55–M59. In the console output, M50 fails `invariant_I1_relativeConservation` ("settled although the forward leg debited PayLink less"), M53 `invariant_I6_exactDeltas` ("payee delta != amount"), M55 `invariant_I9_onlyPayeeCancels` ("succeeded for a caller other than the payee (tx.origin was the payee)") and M57 `invariant_I3_totalsMatchPaidEvents` (`Paid` names the origin) and `invariant_I6_exactDeltas` (payer delta); each also fails `invariant_ModelAgreesWithContract`. See [README.md](README.md#mutation-testing) and `mutation/results.json`.

## What the campaign does not cover

- **Error precedence** between signature checks and state: the campaign only submits invalid payee signatures through `crossDomainReplay`, whose key is never cancelled or expired on the domain it is replayed on. Precedence is pinned deterministically by `Precedence.t.sol` instead (see the mapping).
- **Signature forgery** beyond the scenarios above. The campaign uses valid ECDSA signatures from known keys; malformed, high-s, compact, foreign-signer and ERC-1271 edge cases are unit- and fuzz-tested (`Signatures.t.sol`, `Binding.t.sol::testFuzz_HighSNeverVerifies`, `testFuzz_RandomBytesNeverVerify`, `testFuzz_OtherSignerNeverVerifies`). The Halmos checks ([properties.md](properties.md)) target conservation and the state machine only, with the signatures stubbed, and are never presented as a proof about signatures.
- **Rebasing tokens** are unsupported by design (spec §3.3.3); `Rebasing` is exercised in unit tests only.
- **Real tokens.** The mocks reproduce FiatToken v2.2's observable 3009 and 2612 behaviour (strict time bounds, random nonces, `to == msg.sender`, low-s only, its revert strings). Real USDC, AUSD and MUSD are to be checked by the nightly fork tests (spec §4.3), which are *planned (T1)* and do not exist yet.
- **Gas exhaustion** by a hostile ERC-1271 payee is covered by `Signatures.t.sol` (`Wallet1271Gas`: burn-all-gas, return bomb, short return, wrong magic, revert): on-chain, such a payee only breaks payments to itself. It can still make a relayer's simulated call revert on inclusion, at the relayer's expense; that is pinned by `test/audit/A02_RelayerGriefing.t.sol` and handled by the relayer's admission policy ([THREAT_MODEL T-13](../../docs/security/THREAT_MODEL.md#t-13)).
