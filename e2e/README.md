# @paylink/e2e

Playwright end-to-end tests (PAYLINK-V2-SPEC §4.2): the v2 web app (`apps/web`, [ADR 0014](../docs/adr/0014-web-app-and-single-pages-site.md)) as Cloudflare Pages serves it, and the browser deploy page, `web/v2/deploy/` ([ADR 0013](../docs/adr/0013-browser-deploy-page.md)). The T1 flows of §4.2 (relayer, passkeys, indexer) join them with their features.

| Spec | What it proves |
|---|---|
| `specs/deploy.spec.ts` | Monad testnet 10143 (anvil's Monad emulation, 100 gwei base fee, 5 MON): CREATE2 through the proxy installed by its canonical presigned transaction; the one transaction the wallet receives is Deploy.s.sol's, with the clamped gas limit; reads go to the registry RPC; the record equals `tools/verify-deployment`'s; copy and download. Base Sepolia 84532: the wallet does not know the chain and adds it from the registry (proxy via `anvil_setCode`). Arbitrum Sepolia 421614 without the proxy: CREATE at the predicted address, then resume after a reload. Refusals: unknown chain, tampered release data, foreign code at the proxy address, empty wallet, declined signature. Phone (dark): with a chain chosen from a link (`?chain=monad`) at 320 and 390 px, and through the deployment at 390 px, no horizontal scroll and no word broken inside itself. axe-core WCAG 2.2 AA: zero violations, light and dark |
| `specs/app.spec.ts` | The web app's production build (`apps/web/dist-e2e`, edition `all`, built by the spec) under its real `_headers`, served by `fixtures/pages.ts`, against anvil on 10143 with the PayLinkV2 release at its CREATE2 address, a 6-decimal EIP-2612 + EIP-3009 dollar and an 18-decimal EIP-2612-only dollar (`fixtures/app.ts`). Hero flow: the payee signs an invoice (one `eth_signTypedData_v4`, no transaction; while reviewing, the signature is the only laterite key), shares it (QR code, WhatsApp link, the link printed in full on paper and as a short form with a visible ellipsis on screen); the receive card prints on one A6 page; the payer, on another network, sees four lamps, switches network and, with the fee service down, pays with one EIP-3009 authorisation sent from the wallet as `payWithAuthorization` (exactly one transaction, explicit gas limit, exactly the amount moved), gets a receipt that re-verifies on chain and prints on an 80 mm roll and on A6 (PDF MediaBox); the link then refuses a second payment; the payee's ledger shows it paid, with the total and a CSV export. Focus rings and selected-state marks reach 3:1 in both themes. The EIP-2612-only dollar pays with `payWithPermit`. The armed till stays dark for another invoice and lights for its own: a verdict band with a check glyph and PAID at 48 px or more, and a full screen that keeps only the window and its keys. Cancel from the ledger. An impostor contract at the registry address (chain 84532 in this build) turns the genuine lamp red and locks Pay. Refusals: a changed amount, another chain, a wrong memo, trailing data, an oversized link, the payee's own invoice. Framing: blocked by the header, and locked by the JavaScript frame lock when the header is removed. EN → FR → MG. Every route in EN, FR and MG, light and dark, at 1280, 768, 390 and 320 px: axe-core WCAG 2.2 AA zero violations (not at 768), no horizontal scroll, no footer link drawn over another or over its label, no word broken inside itself, no copy cut by an ellipsis (`fixtures/layout.ts`). No CSP violation under Trusted Types; the service worker installs and the shell opens offline. Each area's headers, and v1 under `/arc/` byte for byte |
| `specs/editions.spec.ts` | The editions (`apps/web/dist-e2e-editions`: `all`, `monad`, `base`, built by the spec) under their real `_headers`, against two anvil chains with the release at its CREATE2 address (10143 with Mock3009 as AUSD and `MockAusdFaucet`; 84532 with Mock3009 as USDC), **the relayer itself** (`apps/relayer` `start:local` in its own process, key derived from anvil's public mnemonic; the production relayer origin of `/config.json` is routed to it) and **Chromium's WebAuthn virtual authenticator with PRF** (`fixtures/editions.ts`). Monad: the seller creates a PayLink key and signs an invoice behind the signing display; the payer, on a phone, creates their own key, gets test AUSD through the relayer's faucet onboarding, pays with one fingerprint through `payWithAuthorization` and never holds MON; the receipt verifies; the armed till lights; the seller cancels another invoice gaslessly (`cancelBySig`); a receive card on Send is paid by a contact for an amount they type. Base: an EOA pays USDC with one EIP-3009 signature and no transaction; a smart account (EIP-5792 `atomic: supported`) pays with "Pay with Base", one `wallet_sendCalls([approve(amount), pay])`. `all`: the relayer refuses the payment after the signature; "Pay with my own MON fee" sends the same authorisation (one `payWithAuthorization`, no second signature), and after a reload with the relayer gone the stored authorisation is resubmitted. The KeyCard on a 360 × 740 phone: the test-network warning before the key that creates the account, the close key always in view. Malagasy at 320 px through the KeyCard, the create form, the signing display, the issued card and the pay view: no word broken inside itself, nothing cut, no sideways scroll, axe-core zero violations (dark) |
| `specs/record-parity.spec.ts` | Foundry deploys and writes the record; the page (verify-only, then with the transaction hash) and the CLI print the same bytes |

The deploy page is served from `web/` with the response headers of `web/_headers`; the app from its build with its own `_headers`, by a server that follows Cloudflare Pages' routing and header rules (`fixtures/pages.ts`: the nearest `404.html`, `! Name` detaches, a header set twice is joined with a comma). The registry's RPC URLs are routed to the anvil running that chain id; the mock wallet (`fixtures/wallet.ts`) is announced through EIP-6963 and forwards to anvil, whose default accounts are unlocked. No real key is used.

```bash
source ~/.paylink-toolchain/env.sh                     # sandbox: anvil/forge on PATH, browsers in /opt/pw-browsers
pnpm --filter @paylink/e2e test                         # needs `forge build` in protocol/ (release build for the parity spec, Mock3009 for the app spec)
```

Screenshots of the review and done states (1280 px light, 390 px dark) are written to `e2e/test-results/` (git-ignored).
